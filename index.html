How to play

Goal

Seat exactly one cat in every row, every column and every colored box. Cats want their space: no two may touch, not even at a corner.

Controls

  • Tap an empty cell once for a paw print: "no cat here".
  • Tap again to seat a cat.
  • Tap a third time to clear the cell.
  • Drag across cells to paw (or erase) many at once.
  • Faint paw prints appear around your cats by themselves: those cells are ruled out.

Grandma Cat

If cats are clashing, she points them out. If not, she rules out one cell for you.
